Aethel trip runs group trips from Bengaluru for people who would rather not go alone. You pick a date, a host meets you at the pick-up point on Friday night, and by Saturday morning you are on the coast with a group of people who are about to be friends. Gokarna is the first trip: two nights, a kayak on a calm sea, a sunset on the rocks and a real party on the sand after dark, with a bonfire to finish. Meera hosts both dates. It costs ₹6,499 and covers the bus, the beach stay, breakfast and dinner. Nothing is charged until we confirm your seat. Say you're in.
