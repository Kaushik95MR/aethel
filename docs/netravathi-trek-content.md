**Description**

Netravathi Peak rises above Samse village in the Chikkamagaluru hills, near Kudremukh. The trail leaves the forest checkpost, crosses a stream and climbs through open shola grassland with several false summits before the top. From the summit you look across to Kudremukh and the Rani Jhari ridge. We travel overnight from Bengaluru, trek on Saturday with a local guide, sleep in a homestay and return on Sunday.

## 4. Facts to confirm before publishing

These came from trek operators and guides, not from an official forest department page.

- **Summit height:** about 1,520 m, per [Indiahikes](https://www.indiahikes.com/documented-trek/netravati-trek) and [TrekUp](https://www.trekupindia.com/?p=6099).
- **Distance:** sources disagree. Indiahikes says 6.4 km round trip, while [BMC Adventures](https://www.bmcadventures.com/tours/netravathi-peak-trek-chikmagalur-100698) and others say 12 to 16 km. Use your guide's figure.
- **Start point:** Samse village, about 14 km from Kalasa (Indiahikes).
- **Guide is required**, usually arranged through the homestay. Indiahikes quotes ₹1,500 per group of up to 10.
- **Permit:** through the Aranya Vihara portal, Dakshina Kannada district (Indiahikes). [Bikat Adventures](https://www.bikatadventures.com/Home/Blog/a-diy-guide-to-the-netravati-trek) says camping is not allowed, which is why this plan uses a homestay.
- **Season:** sources differ. TrekUp suggests post-monsoon and winter for clear views. Monsoon brings leeches and a harder stream crossing.
- **Difficulty:** rated easy to moderate by most operators. Indiahikes says beginners can attempt it.
- **2026 trail status:** one blog says the trail reopened under new booking rules, but I found no official confirmation. Check with the forest department.
